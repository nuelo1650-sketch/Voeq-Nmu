1 · What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. It is sent back to the site on later visits, which lets the site remember who you are and what you were doing. We also use local storage, which works the same way but stays on your device until you clear it.

We use cookies on register.voeq.ng and on voeq.ng. The marketplace and the Register share a cookie for signed-in sessions, so that you are signed in once across both.

2 · How we use them

We use cookies for four purposes, and nothing else:

  1. to keep you signed in;
  2. to remember where you left off in an application, so you can finish it later;
  3. to count how the Register is used, in aggregate;
  4. to measure whether marketplace advertising reaches students.

We do not use cookies to build an advertising profile of individual students, we do not sell data to advertising networks, and we do not allow third-party advertising networks to set their own cookies on the Register.

3 · Strictly necessary

These cookies make the Register work. They are set without asking, because without them you could not apply or check an application. Refusing them does not remove any other feature of the site.

Name Purpose Duration
nmu_session Keeps you signed in while you apply or check an entry Until you sign out
nmu_draft Remembers your in-progress application so you can finish later 30 days
nmu_magic Verifies the sign-in link sent to your email or phone 15 minutes
nmu_register_consent Records that you accepted these terms and this policy 12 months
nmu_csrf Protects your forms against cross-site request forgery Session

4 · Analytics

We count page views and the parts of the Register people actually read, so that we can improve it. These cookies are set only if you accept them, and the measurements are aggregated. You can still use the whole site without them.

Name Purpose Duration
voeq_analytics Counts page views and reads, in aggregate 13 months
voeq_anon_id Distinguishes one visit from another without identifying you 13 months

5 · Marketing

These cookies measure whether marketplace advertising reaches students, so that the Department and Voeq can see which campaigns work. They are set only if you accept them.

Name Purpose Duration
voeq_retargeting Measures whether marketplace campaigns reach students 90 days
voeq_campaign Remembers which campaign sent you to the marketplace 90 days

6 · How long they last

Strictly necessary cookies last for the length of your session, or up to 30 days for a saved draft. Your consent record lasts 12 months, after which we ask again. Analytics and marketing cookies are as set out in the tables above.

You can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop you from signing in and from applying.

7 · Managing your choices

You chose your cookie preferences on the banner that appears the first time you visit. You can change them at any time by clearing the nmu_register_consent cookie in your browser, which will bring the banner back.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser's settings. Your browser's "Do Not Track" setting may also prevent analytics cookies being set.

8 · Global Privacy Control

We honour Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends that signal, we treat it as a refusal of analytics and marketing cookies, and we do not set them.

9 · Changes to this policy

If we add a cookie or change a purpose, we will update this page and the banner, and we will ask for consent again where the law requires it. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
