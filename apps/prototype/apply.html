06

Declaration and consent

Read these, then confirm and submit.

A. Accuracy of information

  • All information submitted is accurate and complete.
  • Uploaded flyers, images and supporting materials genuinely represent the business.
  • The applicant is authorised to submit the application on behalf of the business.

B. Compliance with university requirements

  • The business is expected to comply with applicable university rules and regulations.
  • The Media Department reserves the right to determine whether a business qualifies for registration.
  • Registration may be withdrawn if information supplied is false or the business violates applicable requirements.

C. Registration and public listing

  • The application must be approved before any payment is made.
  • Successful registration results in the business being included in the University's business directory on Voeq.
  • Relevant business information — name, category, description, promotional materials and appropriate contact details — may be displayed publicly.
  • Personal information and administrative documents submitted during registration are not displayed publicly.
Nothing is charged today
Applying is free. If the Media Department approves your application, you will receive the payment instructions on your own status page and by email.
