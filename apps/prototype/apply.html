Declaration and consent

Read these, then confirm and submit.

A. Accuracy of information
  • All information submitted is accurate and complete.
  • Uploaded flyers, images and supporting materials genuinely represent the business.
  • The applicant is authorised to submit the application on behalf of the business.
B. Compliance with university requirements
  • The business is expected to comply with applicable university rules and regulations.
  • The Media Department reserves the right to review applications and determine whether businesses qualify for registration.
  • Registration may be withdrawn if information supplied is false or the business violates applicable requirements.
C. Registration and public listing
  • The application must be approved before payment is made through the registration process.
  • Successful registration will result in the business being included in the university's business directory on Voeq.
  • Relevant business information, including name, category, description, promotional materials and appropriate contact details, may be displayed publicly.
  • Personal information and administrative documents submitted during registration will not automatically be displayed publicly.
