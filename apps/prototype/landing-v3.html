Before you start

Have these ready.

  • ACAC certificate — or business name reservation if still in progress
  • BPassport photo of the owner — phone camera is fine
  • CA photo of the business — shop, stall or workspace
  • DA phone number and email — for the decision letter

On a phone? The whole application works on one, and photos are compressed before upload to save your data.
