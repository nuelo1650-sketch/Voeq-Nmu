Before you start

Have these ready and it goes faster.

  • ACAC registration certificate — or your business name reservation, if registration is still in progress.
  • BA passport photo of the owner — a clear front-facing photo, phone camera is fine.
  • CA photo of the business — your shop, stall or workspace as it stands.
  • DA contact that reaches you — phone number and email, for the decision letter.
