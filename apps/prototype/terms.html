1 · About these terms

These Terms of Service govern your use of the NMU Business Register (the Register), the online register of businesses approved by the Media Department of Nigeria Maritime University (the Department). The Register is operated by the Department together with Voeq Nigeria Limited (Voeq), which provides the platform on which the Register runs.

By submitting an application, or by using the Register, you agree to these terms.

In these terms:

  • "Applicant" means the person who submits an application, whether or not they are the owner of the business.
  • "Applicant Data" means the personal information you provide when applying or using the Register.
  • "Business Information" means the information about a business that appears in the public Register.
  • "Registration Fee" means the fee payable for a registered entry, as set out on your status page.
  • "we", "us" and "our" mean the Department, together with Voeq where we operate the platform.

2 · The Register

The Register is the Department's record of businesses it has approved to operate on campus. The Register is not a company register, a business registry, or a licence to trade, and entry in the Register does not grant any legal status, incorporation, or right to operate beyond the permissions the University grants separately.

The Register is published at register.voeq.ng and entries are also listed on voeq.ng.

3 · Who may apply

Any student, staff member, or recent graduate of Nigeria Maritime University may apply for a business they own or manage. You may apply on behalf of a business you are authorised to represent, and you must say so on the application.

You must be at least 18 years old to enter into a binding agreement under these terms. Applications from business owners who are not eligible may be refused at assessment.

4 · What you must provide

You must provide accurate, complete and current information. In particular you must:

  1. Give your real name, a telephone number the Department can reach you on, and an email address if you have one.
  2. Give the business name, category, description and campus location accurately.
  3. Upload only material that genuinely represents the business and that you have the right to upload.
  4. Say how you are connected to the business, and tell us if that changes.
  5. Tell us promptly if any material you have uploaded becomes inaccurate or is withdrawn.

If any information you give is false, incomplete, or misleading, we may refuse the application, and we may withdraw the entry if the problem is discovered later.

5 · How we assess an application

Every application is read by a named officer of the Media Unit. We assess whether the business is consistent with the University's rules, whether the materials represent the business, and whether the entry can be published. We do not use automated scoring to decide applications.

We will tell you the outcome. If we need changes, we will say specifically what needs to change and why, and you may correct the entry and resubmit it without a new application. Your reference number does not change when you resubmit.

We aim to reach a decision within five working days of a complete submission. This is a target, not a guarantee, and periods around public holidays may be longer.

6 · Fees and payment

Applying is free. A Registration Fee is payable only if your application is approved. The amount, the account to pay, and the reference you must use appear on your own status page at the time you are asked to pay.

Payment is made by direct transfer to the University's nominated account. We do not collect, hold, or take custody of the money. When you pay, you must use the reference number shown on your status page so that your payment can be matched to your entry.

We confirm a payment by matching it against the University's bank record. Until we have done that, your entry is not registered and you will not appear in the Register. If a payment is not recognised, we will contact you and you can resubmit your receipt; you will not be asked to pay twice.

7 · Listing and public information

If your entry is approved and the Registration Fee is confirmed, the following will be published in the Register: the business name, its category, its campus, the description you gave, your promotional material, and any contact details you asked us to publish.

The following will not be published: your phone number, your email address, the payment instructions, the declaration you signed, any correspondence with the Department, and any document you uploaded for assessment other than your promotional material.

Your entry stays published until it is removed under clause 10. If you want your entry removed from the public Register, contact the Department; removal from the Register is not the same as withdrawal of the entry, and we may need to confirm your identity before we remove it.

8 · Your information

We collect the Applicant Data you give us, the material you upload, and a record of every decision taken on your application. We use it to assess the application, to administer the entry, to contact you, to keep the Department's audit trail, and to operate and improve the Register.

Our staff see your Applicant Data only as far as they need to in order to do their job. Decisions are recorded against the name of the officer who took them.

Your full rights over your personal information are set out in the Privacy Policy.

9 · Changing an entry

You may change your business information at any time by contacting the Department, or by editing the entry if the Department has asked you for a correction. We may ask you to re-verify material before a change is published.

A change of business name, category, or campus is treated as a material change and may require a fresh assessment.

10 · Removal, suspension and withdrawal

The Department may withdraw or suspend an entry where:

  1. information supplied was false, or the materials did not represent the business;
  2. the business no longer meets the University's requirements for operating on campus;
  3. the business breaches the University's rules or the law;
  4. the business owner asks us to withdraw the entry.

Where we suspend or withdraw an entry, we will tell you the reason in writing and tell you what you can do about it. Withdrawal of an entry does not entitle you to a refund of a Registration Fee already paid.

11 · Intellectual property

You keep ownership of the promotional material you upload. You grant the Department and Voeq a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display that material as part of your entry in the Register and on voeq.ng, for as long as the entry is published.

You must not upload material that belongs to someone else, or that infringes anyone's rights. You confirm that you have the right to upload everything you submit.

The Department's name, marks and the design of the Register remain its property and may not be reproduced without permission.

12 · Liability

We take care to keep the Register accurate, but we do not warrant that it is complete, that it is error-free, or that a business in the Register is endorsed by the University beyond its appearance in it. Nothing in the Register is advice, and no business is approved by the University as to the quality, safety or legality of what it sells or does.

To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for loss arising from your use of the Register or your reliance on information published in it, including loss of profit, loss of business, or loss of opportunity. Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

13 · Complaints and disputes

If you disagree with a decision, write to the Department and ask for it to be reconsidered. A decision will be reviewed by a different officer from the one who made it, and you will receive the outcome in writing.

If the matter is not resolved, you may raise it with the Registrar of the University. We would rather hear from you than have you take your business elsewhere without telling us why.

14 · Personal data

The processing of your personal information is described in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these terms.

15 · Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep you signed in, to remember where you left off in an application, and to understand how the Register is used. You can refuse non-essential cookies without losing the ability to apply. The Cookie Policy lists every cookie we set and why.

16 · Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. If a change affects you materially — for example the fee, or what is published — we will tell you by email and, where an entry is concerned, show the change on your status page before it takes effect. Changes do not apply to applications already submitted until the entry has been decided.

17 · Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, and the courts of Nigeria have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them. If any provision of these terms is held to be unenforceable, the rest of the terms continue to apply.
