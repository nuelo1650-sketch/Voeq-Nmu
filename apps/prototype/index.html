How it works

Four steps, and you always know where you stand.

1
Apply

Six short sections about you and your business. Save and come back any time.

2
The Department reviews

The Media Department assesses your application and approves, or asks for a correction.

3
Pay

If approved, you get the payment instructions and upload your receipt here.

4
You're listed

Once payment is confirmed, you appear in the NMU directory with an NMU Registered badge.

Before you start

What you'll need

Gather these and the whole thing takes about ten minutes.

Start an application →
  • Your details
    Full name, phone number, and how you relate to the business.
  • Your business
    Name, category, what you do, and where you operate.
  • A flyer or photos
    Anything that represents the business. JPG, PNG or PDF.
  • Links, if you have them
    A website, Instagram page or portfolio. Optional for most businesses.
Fees

One registration fee, one platform fee.

You only pay after the Media Department approves your application. Nothing is charged to apply, and you will see the exact breakdown and where to send it before you pay anything.
