How an entry is made

Four steps · about twelve minutes

The old process was a letter, a stamped form and a wait. This is the same decision, made by the same office, with a record you can see.

You will know where your entry is at every stage, and you will get an email when something changes.

No.StepTime
01 Make the entry ~12 min Six short sections about you and your business. Save and come back.
02 The Department reads it 5 days A named officer at the Media Unit assesses the entry against the University's requirements.
03 A decision, in writing same day Approved, or a correction is requested. Either way you are told what to change and why.
04 Settle the fee 1–3 days Transfer to the University's account and upload the receipt. A finance officer confirms it against the bank record.

What an entry needs

Gather these first

Everything below can be gathered from a phone in one sitting. Nothing here needs a company registration certificate — the Media Department registers student and staff businesses, not companies.

Register your business →

You can stop at any point and finish later on the same device.

01

Your details

Full name, a phone number the Department can reach you on, and how you are connected to the business.

02

Your business

The name you trade under, the category that fits it best, what it does, and where it operates on campus.

03

A statement of business purpose

What the business is for, what it sells or does, and what it gives back to the university community.

04

Something to represent it

A flyer, a photograph of the shop, or a menu. This becomes your listing image — it has to show the business name.

05

Links, if you have them

A website, an Instagram page, a WhatsApp business number. Optional, but it helps the Department judge a trading business.

Fees

Applying is free. If your entry is approved, a registration fee applies — the exact amount and where to pay it appear on your own status page.

What this means

Nothing is charged to apply, and nothing is charged to have your entry assessed. A fee arises only if the Department approves you, and the instructions you need — account details, amount, and reference — are given to you on your own status page.

If your entry is not approved, there is nothing to pay.
