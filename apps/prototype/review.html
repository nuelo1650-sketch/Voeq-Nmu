Statement of business purpose

Ade Tailoring House is a student-run tailoring business operating from Market Row on the Kurutie campus. We make and sell ready-made shirts, trousers and simple traditional outfits at student prices, and we offer alteration and fitting services for clothes bought elsewhere. We hold fittings twice a week and take walk-ins the rest of the time. We employ four students part-time and intend to take on more as the business grows through the semester.
